Free Shopify email template designer

Design a branded order confirmation, shipping confirmation or refund email, watch it update as you type, then copy a template you paste into Shopify under Settings › Notifications. Free, nothing to install, no signup. It uses Shopify's own email variables, so every order fills in by itself.

Colors, font, logo and footer apply to all three emails.

Use it in your store
  1. Click Copy template.
  2. In Shopify admin go to Settings › Notifications › Customer notifications and open , then Edit code.
  3. Select all of the existing code, paste, and click Save. Use Send test email to see it in your inbox.

Keep a copy of Shopify's original code first if you might want it back (Shopify also has Revert to default). Want to change the design later without pasting again? The Sendwell app for Shopify (coming soon) keeps your design in your store and updates every email from one screen.

What the template does

It is plain Shopify Liquid built on Shopify's notification variables (line_items, order_name, shipping_address, fulfillment.tracking_numbers, refund_line_items and the rest). Every order fills in its own items, prices, address and links. Nothing is loaded from outside Shopify, and the email works in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail with table-based layout and inline styles.
